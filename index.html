About Fresh Feel Painting & Cleaning

Fresh Feel was founded with a simple yet powerful vision: to revolutionize how people experience home and commercial space transformations. Born from years of industry expertise and a passion for excellence, our company emerged from the realization that homeowners and business owners deserved a single, trusted partner who could deliver both exceptional painting and thorough cleaning services. What started as a small local operation has grown into a respected name in the community, trusted by hundreds of satisfied clients who have witnessed their spaces transform into beautiful, well-maintained environments.

At Fresh Feel, we're driven by core values of integrity, craftsmanship, and customer satisfaction. We believe that every project, regardless of size, deserves meticulous attention to detail and professional execution. Our mission is to exceed expectations by combining superior painting techniques with comprehensive cleaning services, ensuring that every client receives a complete, hassle-free transformation. We're committed to using quality materials, employing skilled professionals, and maintaining transparent communication throughout every project. Your satisfaction isn't just our goal—it's the foundation of everything we do.

The timeline for your painting project depends on several factors including the size of the space, number of rooms, surface condition, and the complexity of the color scheme. Most residential projects take between 3-7 days. A single bedroom typically takes 1-2 days, while an entire home might take 1-2 weeks. We always provide a detailed timeline estimate during the initial consultation so you know exactly what to expect. Our efficient team works diligently to complete projects on schedule without compromising quality.

Our comprehensive color consultation includes an in-person assessment of your space, analysis of lighting conditions and existing décor, recommendations for color palettes that match your style and preferences, and sample testing on your walls. We discuss trending colors as well as timeless options that will maintain their appeal for years to come. Our color experts consider factors like room size, natural light, furniture, and the overall mood you want to create. We'll work with you until you're completely satisfied with your color choice before we begin painting.
